What is Nueva Andalucía?

Nueva Andalucía is the valley that opens up behind Puerto Banús, within the municipality of Marbella. It is known as the Golf Valley because it holds three 18-hole courses within the valley itself: Real Club de Golf Las Brisas, Aloha Golf Club and Los Naranjos Golf Club. It is not a single gated estate but a collection of developments with their own shops, schools and restaurants.

Where exactly is it?

The valley sits between Marbella centre and San Pedro de Alcántara, rising from the coast towards the mountains. Puerto Banús is at its southern end, on the beach, and the main entrance is taken from the A-7 at that point.

The three golf courses

Los Naranjos Golf Club opened in 1977 with a Robert Trent Jones Senior layout. Real Club de Golf Las Brisas and Aloha Golf Club complete the triangle, all three within a few minutes of each other by car. It is the densest concentration of courses around Marbella and explains much of the housing demand in the valley.

Schools and daily life

Aloha College, founded in 1982, is inside the valley and is one of the reasons many international families choose this area over the seafront. The Centro Plaza and Aloha area holds supermarkets, clinics and offices, and the Saturday market is a fixture of the local calendar.

What people buy here

The offer runs from apartments in landscaped developments with communal pools, very common around the courses, to detached villas on the valley slopes with views of La Concha or the sea. It is one of the few parts of Marbella where both products live side by side in the same postcode.
