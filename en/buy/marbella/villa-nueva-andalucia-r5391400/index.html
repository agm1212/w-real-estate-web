This exceptional contemporary villa offers a refined lifestyle in a prestigious frontline golf setting in Nueva Andalucía, Marbella. Designed with architectural simplicity, warm natural finishes and sophisticated details, the residence combines modern elegance, privacy and breathtaking views over the golf course and surrounding mountains.

The villa showcases a harmonious blend of clean contemporary lines, elegant oak detailing, premium stone surfaces and expansive floor-to-ceiling glazing. The carefully designed interiors are filled with natural light, creating a seamless connection between the indoor and outdoor spaces while maintaining a warm and inviting atmosphere throughout the home.

The spacious open-plan living area brings together the lounge, dining area and designer kitchen into one elegant social space. The bespoke kitchen features custom cabinetry and a striking central island, creating both a practical everyday environment and an impressive setting for entertaining.
