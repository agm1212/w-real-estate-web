This exceptional semi-detached villa is a rare gem in the prestigious beachfront development of La Perla de la Bahía, located right next to the exclusive Finca Cortesin Beach Club. One of only three villas in the entire community, the rest being apartments, this home offers the unique feel of a private villa with direct access to the beach and promenade—where chic beachside restaurants are just a short walk away.

Set within its own wrap-around garden of nearly 300 m², complete with a private swimming pool, this residence combines privacy and exclusivity with the benefits of a secure community, which also offers a beautiful communal pool.

The villa is distributed across three levels:

Ground floor: Elegant hallway, spacious family kitchen, bright living room opening onto a covered terrace for year-round outdoor living, plus a guest bedroom suite.

First floor: A magnificent master suite and an additional guest bedroom, both with access to a large terrace boasting stunning sea views.

Lower level: Two versatile rooms, a large utility area (with potential to add a bathroom), and a private garage with space for at least 3 cars.

Built in the signature colorful, rustic Andalusian style that La Perla de la Bahía is famous for, the property features high-quality materials, including underfloor heating on the ground floor and in all bathrooms, ensuring year-round comfort.

This villa represents a rare investment opportunity—frontline beach homes of this size and privacy are seldom available in this price range.
